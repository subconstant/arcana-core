--- --- Arcana

⟡ Arcana is a portal for new forms and methods in online and decentralized art. ⟡

⟡ Spawned in a constellation of offsite, onchain, and online art projects, we embrace a memetic, hivemind-driven approach to creation - generating and compositing from the living materials of evolving meme and otaku tropes, gamespace artifacts, AI dreaming, and the recursive layers of culture and nature. ⟡

⟡ Exhibitions and events are curated, or grow in group collaboration, with a preference for aesthetics, intuition, and spiritual modes in art. We also post and feature projects from our friends and network. ⟡

⟡ Founded by c_hristian, website made in collaboration with em. Additional conceptual and spiritual help provided by the Arcana community. ⟡
